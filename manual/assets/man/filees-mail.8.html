NAME

filees-mail — submit one FileES outbox entry or drain Public Shares mail

SYNOPSIS

filees-mail [-config path] send

filees-mail [-config path] public-loop

DESCRIPTION

send claims one pending control-plane mail job and submits it through the configured SMTP relay. It is the one-shot drain used after filees-admin(8) ticket actions and after filees-bootstrap-entry(8). Onboarding, realm-removal, data-erasure and upload-shelf invitation jobs share this path.

Delivery states are pending, sending, queued, and failed. A 4xx leaves the job pending and preserves the OTP. A 5xx marks an onboarding job failed and redacts the OTP. An upload-shelf invitation 5xx marks the outbox job failed without retry, so public-loop does not hammer the relay. The invitation token remains on the channel record. The Message-ID is derived from CreatedAt, so a lost 250 can be retried safely.

public-loop is not an operator command. filees-public-authority(8) supervises it as an isolated child. The child loads the SMTP secret and drains the Public Shares outbox and the upload-shelf invitation outbox. The authority process never receives that secret. If the child dies, the authority exits.

EXIT STATUS

0
accepted (queued) or nothing pending
75
temporary SMTP failure; retry
69
permanent SMTP failure; the job is marked failed, not retried
65
message render failure; the job is marked failed, not retried
64, 78, 70
usage, configuration or internal error

EXAMPLES

Retry a 4xx invitation:

$ doas -u _filees-state filees-mail send
